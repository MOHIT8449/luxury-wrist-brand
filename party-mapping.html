Tally Party Mapping

Every Tally party is one row. Next to it, choose its Main party from the ERP list (e.g. PRIYA TRADERS → SHREE JAGDISH TRADERS, ROHIT TRADERS → SHREE JAGDISH TRADERS). It saves by itself. Do it once; after that Tally Bill & Invoice No., Tally Bill Check and the Cashbook keep all these bills under the main party, in one ledger.

Tally parties → Main party

Add the Tally party list

Paste the party names from Tally or Excel (one per line), or choose the Tally List of Ledgers / Sales Register file. Names already in the list are not added twice. Add more any time later.

Or type a main party once and all its Tally sub-firms below it (one per line).

Excel

Export Excel gives every Tally party with its main party. Fill or change the "Main Party (ERP)" column in Excel, and import it back. Rows with an empty main party are left as they are.
