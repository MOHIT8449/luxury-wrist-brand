Tally Party Mapping

Map each Tally bill party (sub-firm, e.g. PRIYA TRADERS) to its main ERP party (e.g. SHREE JAGDISH TRADERS) once. After that the Firm list on Tally Bill & Invoice No., Tally Bill Check and the Cashbook all keep the money under the main party, with one ledger.

Add sub-firms to a main party

Type the main party, then the sub-firm names as in Tally (one per line).
