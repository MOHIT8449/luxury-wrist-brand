Corporate Background

Luxury Wrist Brand, founded by Mr. Mohit Vaghela in 2018, is a Surat-based multi-category trading and distribution company. The business began with offline retail operations focused on branded wristwatches but rapidly gained customer trust and market credibility. Leveraging this momentum, the company was formally registered as Luxury Wrist Brand and strategically expanded into leading e-commerce marketplaces including Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Snapdeal, Ajio, and others.

What began as a timepiece-focused venture has evolved into a diversified and growth-driven enterprise. Over the years, the company has strategically expanded its portfolio to include cosmetics, personal care, grooming products, and multiple in-house as well as partner brands. This expansion has enabled Luxury Wrist Brand to build a strong and competitive presence across both online and offline distribution channels, reinforcing its position as a trusted multi-category business in the consumer goods industry.

Business Bio

Mohit Vaghela is the Founder and Proprietor of Luxury Wrist Brand, a multi-category trading and distribution company established in 2018. What began as a modest offline venture specializing in branded watches has evolved into a dynamic and diversified enterprise with a robust presence across both e-commerce and traditional retail channels. Today, Luxury Wrist Brand is recognized for its reliability, category expansion, and commitment to delivering high-quality branded products to a growing customer base, positioning itself as a trusted partner for national and international markets.

Journey & Growth

Mohit began his entrepreneurial journey in the offline retail market, specializing in the sale of branded wristwatches. As demand grew and customer confidence strengthened, he expanded his vision and formally registered his first company under the name Luxury Wrist Brand. This milestone marked the transition from a small-scale retail operation to a structured business entity, enabling the company to expand its reach across major e-commerce platforms and build a strong foundation for future growth.

To further strengthen the company's digital footprint, Mohit launched Estrelo, a proprietary brand focused on high-demand personal care products such as beard oil, hair oil, hair serum, vitamin-C serum, and other grooming essentials. Estrelo quickly gained momentum on leading e-commerce platforms including Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, and others, positioning the company as a competitive and fast-growing player in the online personal care market.

Expansion into Multi-Brand Cosmetic Distribution

As the business continued to grow, Mohit expanded Luxury Wrist Brand into an authorized e-commerce distributor for a wide portfolio of well-known cosmetic and personal care brands. Over time, the company secured distribution rights for more than 21 recognized brands, including Richfeel, Swiss Beauty, Hilary Rhoda, Steel Paris, Kiss Beauty, Glam21, VLCC, Jovees, Nature's Essence, and several others. This diversification strengthened the company's market position and reinforced its reputation as a trusted distribution partner in the beauty and personal care industry.

Offline Super Stockist Operations

After achieving strong results in the online marketplace, Mohit expanded into offline distribution to establish a robust multi-channel business model. Today, Luxury Wrist Brand operates as a Super Stockist for several respected FMCG and personal care brands, including Richfeel, Rajnigandha, Coca-Cola, Denver, Pure Home, Envy, Continental Coffee, and other leading consumer brands. This strategic expansion has significantly strengthened the company's footprint across retail supply chains, wholesale markets, and distributor networks, reinforcing its position as a dependable and growth-driven distribution partner.

Infrastructure & Reliability

Luxury Wrist Brand operates from a well-equipped warehouse facility designed to support efficient storage, safe product handling, multiple loading and unloading bays, and fast supply-chain operations. The setup ensures smooth and timely order dispatch across both online and offline channels. Under the leadership of Mohit Vaghela, the company is guided by four core business values : Honesty in Business, Quality Products, Fast & Reliable Service, and Long-Term Partnerships. These principles form the foundation of the company's reputation and ongoing growth.

Today

With over seven years of consistent growth, Mohit Vaghela has developed Luxury Wrist Brand into a reliable, multi-category, multi-brand enterprise trusted by customers, retailers, distributors, and leading online marketplaces. The company's steady expansion and commitment to quality have positioned it as a dependable partner across diverse segments of the consumer goods industry.

The company continues to strengthen its product portfolio, expand its distribution network, and diversify its brand offerings, with a clear vision of becoming a leading name in India's consumer goods and e-commerce supply segment. Brand Journey & Expansion

1. Watches to E-commerce

The company's first major milestone was its entry into the offline market through the sale of branded wristwatches. Building on early success and growing customer demand, Luxury Wrist Brand soon transitioned to leading online marketplaces, where it began managing high-volume e-commerce operations with increased scale and efficiency.

2. Launch of Estrelo

The company further strengthened its market position by launching its in-house grooming brand, ESTRELO, offering a focused range of personal care products including beard oils, hair oils, hair serums, vitamin-C serums, and an extended men's grooming line. Driven by consistent quality, affordability, and customer trust, ESTRELO quickly emerged as a high-performing brand across multiple e-commerce platforms.

3. Authorised Distribution for 20+ Brands

Luxury Wrist Brand further expanded its capabilities by becoming an authorized e-commerce distributor for several leading beauty and cosmetic brands. The company now represents a strong portfolio of 21 recognized brands, including Richfeel, Swiss Beauty, Hilary Rhoda, Steel Paris, Kiss Beauty, Glam21, VLCC, Jovees, Nature's Essence, and others.

This strategic expansion strengthened the company's industry presence and reinforced its reputation as a trusted distribution partner in the beauty and personal care segment.

4. Offline Super Stockist

The company further diversified its operations by expanding into offline distribution through a Super Stockist model, enabling it to serve a wider retail and wholesale network. Under this vertical, Luxury Wrist Brand manages distribution for several prominent FMCG and cosmetic brands, including Rajnigandha, Coca-Cola (Sprite, Maaza, Coca-Cola, Kinley, Charged), Richfeel, Denver, Envy, Pure Home, Continental Coffee, and multiple other household and personal care brands.

This expansion has significantly strengthened the company's multi channel presence and enhanced its role within India's consumer goods supply chain.
