Finished orders: staff see only today's

When ON, every login except Admin sees open / pending orders as before. A delivered order whose work is finished stays visible until 12 midnight (India time) of that day, then only Admin sees it. Nothing is deleted.

An order counts as finished when all of these are done:

  • Driver reported Delivered or Part delivered
  • Driver payment is verified or rejected (not waiting)
  • No driver return waiting for approval, and approved returned goods are received in the godown
  • Accounts entered the Tally invoice no.

If new work comes up later (for example a new return), the order shows again until that work is finished.
